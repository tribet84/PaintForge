The problem it solves

Four questions every painter answers badly from memory.

🫙

“Do I already own this?”

A built-in catalogue of 850+ paints across Citadel, Vallejo, The Army Painter and Green Stuff World. One tap marks a pot as owned, another sends it to the shopping list.

📉

“Am I about to run out?”

Flag a pot as running low the moment you notice. It joins the shopping list on its own, grouped by brand and ready to send to a shop.

🧾

“How did I paint this?”

Recipes record the process section by section, as ordered steps — basecoat, wash, highlight — with the paints, the technique and your own notes.

🎯

“Can I start this army today?”

Group paints into a list for a miniature or a whole force. PintaMinis tells you whether the shelf can actually finish it.

Readiness, not just a list

Every list and every recipe is checked against your inventory. A paint you have wishlisted counts as missing — it is not on the shelf yet — and one missing pot outranks several running low, because it blocks the job outright.

The recipe says Citadel. You paint with Vallejo.

Every paint's card shows its closest matches in the other brands, graded by perceptual colour distance (CIEDE2000) — the industry's measure of what the eye can actually tell apart. Metallics only ever match metallics, washes only match washes: a gold and a brown can share a colour value and share nothing on a mini.

Graded honestly: Twin · Very close · Approximate — and past that, "no close match" instead of a bad one.

Recipes worth sharing

Publish a recipe and anyone with the link can save it to their account.

  • Bookmarks, not copies. Update a shared recipe and everyone who bookmarked it sees the new version — nobody is stuck on a stale fork.
  • Straight to the shopping list. Opening someone's recipe shows which of its paints you are missing, and adds them in one tap.
  • Photos of the real thing. Give a recipe a cover photo and the list becomes a gallery; open one full screen to see the brushwork.

Built for tutorial makers.

If you publish painting guides, your recipes become something people keep rather than screenshot. Update the scheme once and every follower has it.

Your data is yours

  • Private by default. A recipe becomes readable by others only when you publish it, and you can stop sharing at any time.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • Works offline and catches up when you reconnect.
  • Delete your account from Settings and everything goes with it, including anything you published.

Open source.

Built with Flutter and Firebase. The code, the security rules and the reasoning behind the design decisions are all public.
